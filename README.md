<p align="center">
  A modern classroom and timetable management system for the Faculty of Engineering, <br/>
  General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU), Sri Lanka.
</p>

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📅 **Automated Timetable Generation**
- 🔔 **Notifications** for lecturers & students
- 📄 **Export Options** (PDF & Excel)
- 🔐 **Secure Login & Access Control**
- 🏫 **Classroom & Resource Management**
- 👩‍🏫 **Lecturer & Student Management**
- ⚡ **Clash Detection & Auto-resolution**
- 📊 **Availability Reports**

---

## 🎯 System Goals
- Simplify classroom allocations
- Avoid timetable clashes
- Make schedule sharing smooth for lecturers & students
